Hi! I'm Matej Machevski, a student at FINKI Skopje, constantly working on developing practical IT skills through real, self-driven projects. Although I don't yet have formal work experience in the industry, my involvement in several larger projects has helped me face real-world challenges and solve them through logical thinking, research, and persistence.

I've worked on web applications, API integrations, Figma designs, and have also explored parts of AWS. I have experience with programming languages such as Java, JavaScript, Python, C++, HTML/CSS, and others. Through these projects, I’ve gradually developed a deeper understanding of how more complex systems function, how different parts connect, and how to stay resourceful when things don’t go according to plan.

My interests are constantly evolving – I’m interested in both frontend and backend development, as well as design and user experience. That’s why I consider myself flexible and ready to adapt to different roles and positions, always with a clear goal in mind: to keep learning and improving. I have a solid command of English and communicate easily in team environments, especially when working on collaborative projects.

I'm eager to work in a team-oriented and supportive environment where I can learn from more experienced engineers and gain a better understanding of how large-scale, real-world projects function within a company.
