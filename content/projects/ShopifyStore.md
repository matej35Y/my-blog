This project was built for a local company — Zoja, which offers a wide product range: from faucets, plumbing, and electrical supplies, to home tools, paints, varnishes, and power tools.

I decided to build the site using **Shopify** – a no-coding website builder – because setting up and organizing the products seemed like a challenge, especially with implementing payment methods. Shopify handles most of that in the background, making it much easier for the end user. I think it's a great platform when a small team (or in this case, just me) is handling the entire process from start to finish. Unlimited product uploads and support for large product databases are just a few of Shopify’s advantages.

The biggest challenge was adding each product manually. That included writing product titles, descriptions, specifications, and ensuring the product images followed a consistent visual style that matched the website. Another major challenge was filtering and categorizing the products. Structuring the categories and subcategories can get complicated—especially when you don’t have prior experience or a reference point to guide you.

All images, text, typography, designs and even the logo were created by me.

After all the hard work, I can say I'm proud of this project. It's one of the biggest I’ve worked on so far. It functions properly and brings real value to the business — which was my main goal from the beginning.
